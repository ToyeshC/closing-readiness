# PRODUCT.md — Consult&Co Financial Readiness Tool

## Product Purpose
Financial closing-readiness checker for Dutch SME accountants. Runs 10 deterministic
data-quality checks on Exact Online data before allowing Claude to advise on the books.
Responsible AI guardrail: advisory is blocked if data is dirty.

## Users
Primary: accountant/bookkeeper running this for their SME clients. Comfortable with
financial jargon (DSO, DPO, VAT, AR/AP, GL accounts). Values efficiency, trust, and
professional credibility. Presents results to clients on screen.

Secondary (demo): hackathon judges — needs to impress technically and visually.

## Register
product

## Brand
Consult&Co (consultenco.nl). Dutch financial consultancy. Clean, professional,
confident but not flashy. Warm cream/off-white backgrounds, navy as authority color.

## Tone
Authoritative intelligence meets trusted partner. Like a very senior accountant who
also happens to be excellent at data engineering. Dense but clear. Never chatty.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS templates (Tailwind UI defaults)
- Crypto/fintech neon dark mode
- Overly playful fintech (Monzo, Revolut bright colors)
- Navy + gold "classic finance" cliché

## Strategic principles
- Every screen must be presentable to a client on a projected screen
- The readiness score is the hero — everything else supports it
- AI disclosure is non-negotiable (EU AI Act Art. 13)
- Data before opinion: checks precede advisory
