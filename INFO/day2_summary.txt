════════════════════════
WHAT WAS BUILT
════════════════════════

backend/services/normalizer.py
  Three functions: gl_to_source_line(), invoice_to_source_line(), bank_to_source_line()
  Converts raw pandas dicts → SourceLine Pydantic objects for the engine.
  Also exports _to_date() and _to_float() used by all check modules.

backend/services/readiness_engine.py
  ReadinessEngine(dataset).run() → DataReadinessReport
  Calls all 8 checks, scores the result, sets advice_ready.
  Scoring: 1.0 minus penalties (high=0.20, medium=0.10, low=0.03).
  Any blocker → advice_ready=False regardless of score.

backend/services/checks/suspense.py          BLOCKER
  Flags GL entries on account 1250 (Nog te duiden).

backend/services/checks/todo_discrepancy.py  SEVERITY: high
  Reports files in the "to do" import folder that haven't been imported.
  Data comes from dataset.todo_discrepancies (populated by data_loader).

backend/services/checks/revenue_reconciliation.py  SEVERITY: high
  Compares GL 8xxx revenue vs sales entries total (period-filtered, abs values).

backend/services/checks/capex_opex.py        SEVERITY: medium
  Finds asset-keyword entries (machine, fiets, etc.) in 4xxx accounts above €1,000.

backend/services/checks/bank_coverage.py     SEVERITY: medium
  Checks what % of business days have a bank entry. <90% = warn, <80% = fail.

backend/services/checks/ar_aging.py          SEVERITY: medium
  Matches sales invoices to bank payments by amount. Unmatched invoices >90 days old = overdue AR.

backend/services/checks/timing_differences.py  SEVERITY: medium
  Compares GL "Periode" field (integer 1-12) vs boekdatum.month.
  Mismatch = entry posted to wrong accounting period. (Better than boekdatum vs vervaldatum
  which would flag every normal 30-day invoice as an error.)

backend/services/checks/vat_reconciliation.py  SEVERITY: medium
  Extracts VAT from PDF using re.findall (sums all 4 quarterly amounts).
  Compares to GL btw_bedrag sum. Uses project-relative path to PDF.

README.md
  Project overview, architecture diagram, run instructions, team split.
